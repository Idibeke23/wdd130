Nigeria

Nigeria, officially the Federal Republic of Nigeria, is a federal republic located in West Africa, bordered by Niger to the north, Chad to the northeast, Cameroon to the east, Benin to the west and the Gulf of Guinea in the Atlantic Ocean to the south. It has a population of over 239 million people, making it the most populous country in Africa and the sixth-most populous in the world. Its capital is Abuja which is located in the Federal Capital Territory (F.C.T.). Nigeria has 36 states, each with its capital. Lagos serves as the country's major commercial and industrial hub.
